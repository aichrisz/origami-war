      Rules & controls

      Goal

      Each side holds one core (integrity 2) and three blades pinned to paper cells. Take the enemy core to integrity 0 and you win at once.

      The sheet

      A 5×5 sheet of 25 immutable paper cells. Folding reflects a flap across an integer crease, flips every moved cell over, reverses the flap's layer order and lays it on top. Paper cells never move relative to their units; only the paper does.

      Actions

      • Fold — costs one of your three fold tokens. Legal only if the reflected flap lands inside the stationary sheet and covers part of it.
      • Strike — free. A live unit hits a live enemy in the same stack, or in an orthogonally adjacent stack. Blades are captured; cores lose one integrity.

      Ending

      Core captured wins immediately. Otherwise the match ends at 24 committed actions (or when a side has no legal action at all) and is decided on core integrity, then surviving blades, then controlled top faces, then the seeded initiative, which belongs to the side that opened.

      Controls

      • Tab and arrow keys reach every control; Enter or Space activates.
      • Focus or hover a fold button to see the flap, its destination, its travel direction, the resulting layering and the strike contacts it would create.
      • Undo is offered only while your action is still unanswered: before the machine replies in solo, and before you hand the device over in pass and play. It reverts the whole action.
